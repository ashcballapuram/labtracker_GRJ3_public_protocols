# Cleaved Caspase 3

## Protocol Content

### 📅 Day 1

1. Add AC220 and DOX to cells.


### 📅 Day 2

Incubation Continues


### 📅 Day 3



**Preparing Plates**


1.     Spin plates down at 300 RCF for 5 minutes.

2.     Flick plate

3.     Wash the wells with PBS.

4.     Spin plates down at 300 RCF for 5 minutes.

5.     Flick plates

 

**Fix and Perm Plates**

> 🧪 **Reagent:** 16% Paraformaldehyde (20 mL)

> 🧪 **Reagent:** 100% Methanol (20 mL)

6.     Prepare 4% Paraformaldehyde.

7.     Add 200mL of 4% PFA per well.

8.     Incubate in dark at room temperature for 10 minutes.

9.     Spin plates down at 900 RCF for 5 minutes.

10.  Flick plate

11.  Add 200mL of ice-cold methanol.

**12.  **Incubate in the dark at -20C for at least 30 minutes. **STOP POINT**



### 📅 Day 4

> 🧪 **Reagent:** FACS Buffer (10 mL)

13.  Spin plates down at 900 RCF for 5 minutes.

14.  Flick plate.

15.  Wash with cold FACS Buffer

16.  Spin plates down at 900 RCF for 5 minutes.

17.  Flick plate.


 

> 🧪 **Reagent:** Cleaved Caspase 3 (100 µL)

**Staining for Cleaved-Caspase 3**

18.  Prepare Cleave Caspase 3 antibody at 1:50 dilution.

19.  Add 50mL of antibody to each well.

20.  Incubate for 30 minutes.

21.  Add 150mL of cold FACS Buffer.

22.  Spin plates down at 900 RCF for 5 minutes.

23.  Flick plate.

24.  Resuspend pellet in 200mL of cold FAC Buffer.

25.  The plates are now ready to read on Attune

26.  For each plate set the following parameters on Attune

a.     Create new experiment.

b.     Add 1 group

c.     Add the appropriate wells to group

d.     Set FSC voltage to 100V – 150V.

e.     Set SSC voltage to 350V.

f.      Set VL1 voltage to 360V.
